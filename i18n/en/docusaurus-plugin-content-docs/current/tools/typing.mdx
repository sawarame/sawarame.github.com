## How to Use

1. **Select a Course**:
   Choose between the **1-Minute Course (60s)** and **3-Minute Course (180s)**. The selected course automatically syncs with the URL query parameter (`?course=60` / `?course=180`).
2. **Start & Countdown**:
   Click **Start** to begin a 3-second countdown (`3 → 2 → 1`). As soon as the timer starts, the input field is automatically focused.
3. **Commit IME Conversion and Press Enter**:
   Type and convert Japanese text as usual with your IME, then press **Enter while not composing** to submit and match against the passage.
4. **Prefix Digestion & Easy Correction**:
   Only the longest matching prefix from the start of the remaining passage is digested. Any unmatched tail stays in the input box so you can edit or reconvert it right away and press Enter again.
5. **View Results, Personal Bests & Sharing**:
   When time reaches zero, the results screen immediately displays your total digested characters, CPM (characters per minute), completed passages, and rank. Per-course personal bests are automatically stored in LocalStorage, and you can generate and share a branded score card image (including tool version and device info) via the **Share Results** button.

## Matching Rules & Features

### 1. Committed-Text Evaluation for Real-World Japanese Typing
Instead of judging individual romaji keystrokes, this tool measures your practical ability to type and convert accurate Japanese sentences using your own IME—whether you submit short phrases or longer clauses.

### 2. Normalization, Hyphen/Prolonged-Sound Equivalence & Live Color Preview
- **Live pre-submit color preview**: Before pressing Enter, characters currently in the input box are compared in real time against the passage—matching characters appear in green and mismatched characters appear in red.
- **Half-width/Full-width & `ー`/`-` treated equally**: Alphanumeric characters, ASCII symbols, and spaces match regardless of width (e.g., `A` matches `Ａ`, `100%` matches `１００％`), and the prolonged sound mark `ー` matches hyphen `-` (including full-width `－`).
- **Case-sensitive**: Uppercase and lowercase letters are strictly distinguished (`A` does not match `a`).
- **Exact Japanese matching**: Hiragana, katakana, kanji, and Japanese punctuation are matched strictly, with Unicode `NFC` normalization handling combining diacritics.
- **Japanese Passages Across All Locales**: Because this tool focuses specifically on Japanese IME conversion typing, the built-in passages are always presented in Japanese regardless of the site language.

### 3. Rank Evaluation (CPM Thresholds)
Your rank is determined by your CPM (digested characters per minute):

- **Rank S (200+ CPM)**: Expert level with exceptional conversion speed and accuracy.
- **Rank A (150–199 CPM)**: Advanced level suited for fast-paced professional workflows.
- **Rank B (100–149 CPM)**: Practical business level with steady rhythm and accuracy.
- **Rank C (60–99 CPM)**: Standard level for everyday Japanese text input.
- **Rank D (Under 60 CPM)**: Beginner level—focus on steady, accurate phrase conversion.

:::info[Privacy First (100% Client-Side Processing)]
🔒 All text evaluation and personal best storage (LocalStorage) run entirely within your browser. No typed data or scores are ever transmitted to external servers.
:::
