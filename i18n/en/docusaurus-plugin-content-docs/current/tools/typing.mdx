## Why "Japanese IME Typing"? (How It Differs from Traditional Typing Software)

Most traditional Japanese typing games focus on typing prescribed alphabet (romaji) characters one keystroke at a time. While helpful for memorizing key positions, they leave a gap when it comes to the skills real-world Japanese input actually demands—such as converting kana to kanji, deciding clause boundaries, and selecting and committing conversion candidates.

This tool evaluates the text you actually convert and commit with your everyday Japanese IME against each passage. Rather than measuring raw romaji keystroke speed alone, it trains your practical Japanese typing ability end to end—from forming phrases in your head to converting and committing accurate sentences smoothly.

Furthermore, everyday work such as emails, chat messages, documentation, and software development rarely consists of Japanese text alone; numbers, symbols (`%`, `/`, `-`, `:`, `()`, etc.), and English letters appear constantly throughout. By typing realistic long-form passages that naturally combine all of these elements, you can practice input mode switching and conversion habits for truly practical typing proficiency.

## How to Use

1. **Open the Typing Modal & Select a Course**:
   Click **Start Typing Practice** to open the typing modal, then choose from the **1-Minute (60s)**, **3-Minute (180s)**, **5-Minute (300s)**, or **10-Minute (600s)** course.
2. **Start & Countdown**:
   Turn on Japanese input (IME) before clicking **Start** to begin a 3-second countdown (`3 → 2 → 1`). As soon as the timer starts, the input field is automatically focused. *(During countdown and active play, backdrop clicks and the Esc key are disabled to prevent accidental interruptions; use the top-right close button or **Quit** / **Cancel** to exit.)*
3. **Commit IME Conversion and Press Enter**:
   Type and convert Japanese text as usual with your IME, then press **Enter while not composing** to submit and match against the passage.
4. **Prefix Digestion & Easy Correction**:
   Only the longest matching prefix from the start of the remaining passage is digested. Any unmatched tail stays in the input box so you can edit or reconvert it right away and press Enter again.
5. **View Results, Personal Bests & Sharing**:
   When time reaches zero, any unsubmitted text remaining in the input box is automatically evaluated and counted (matching prefix) before immediately showing the results screen with your total digested characters, CPM (characters per minute), completed passages, and rank. Per-course personal bests are automatically stored in LocalStorage, and you can generate and share a branded score card image (including tool version and device info) via the **Share Results** button.

## Matching Rules & Features

### 1. Committed-Text Evaluation for Real-World Japanese Typing
Evaluation is performed on the finalized text committed through your IME. You can submit short phrases after each clause or convert and commit longer segments at once—using predictive conversion and your usual typing workflow freely.

### 2. Normalization, Hyphen/Prolonged-Sound Equivalence & Live Color Preview
- **Live pre-submit color preview**: Before pressing Enter, characters currently in the input box are compared in real time against the passage—matching characters appear in green and mismatched characters appear in red.
- **Visible half-width spaces in passages**: Half-width spaces in the target passage are marked with a subtle `␣`-style indicator so you can spot them easily while typing.
- **Half-width/Full-width & `ー`/`-` treated equally**: Alphanumeric characters, ASCII symbols, and spaces match regardless of width (e.g., `A` matches `Ａ`, `100%` matches `１００％`), and the prolonged sound mark `ー` matches hyphen `-` (including full-width `－`).
- **Case-sensitive**: Uppercase and lowercase letters are strictly distinguished (`A` does not match `a`).
- **Exact Japanese matching**: Hiragana, katakana, kanji, and Japanese punctuation are matched strictly, with Unicode `NFC` normalization handling combining diacritics.
- **Japanese Passages Across All Locales**: Because this tool focuses specifically on Japanese IME conversion typing, the built-in passages are always presented in Japanese regardless of the site language.

### 3. Rank Evaluation (CPM Thresholds)
Your rank is determined by your CPM (digested characters per minute):

- **Rank S (200+ CPM)**: Expert level with exceptional conversion speed and accuracy.
- **Rank A (150–199 CPM)**: Advanced level suited for fast-paced professional workflows.
- **Rank B (100–149 CPM)**: Practical business level with steady rhythm and accuracy.
- **Rank C (60–99 CPM)**: Standard level for everyday Japanese text input.
- **Rank D (Under 60 CPM)**: Beginner level—focus on steady, accurate phrase conversion.
